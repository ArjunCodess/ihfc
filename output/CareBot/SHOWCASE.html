The route

Optional lane correction is omitted for clarity. Each wall approach stops when the ultrasonic reads 20 cm or less. The beams must be mounted to land correctly from one final stop.

What happens at each stop

  1. First two kits. The ultrasonic reads 20 cm or less from the wall. The robot turns left and opens bin A.
  2. Six kits. Both front IR sensors confirm the black crossing before the middle bin opens.
  3. Last two kits. The robot stops at the next 20 cm threshold and opens bin B.
  4. Beams. It reaches the next wall stop, turns left to release the first beam, then turns left again to release the second.

How it is controlled

An ESP32 commands the L298N motor driver and a PCA9685 board that drives all five positional servos. Connecting the battery starts the route automatically. The ultrasonic sensor measures wall distance, and both front IR sensors confirm black arena tape.

Runtime information

Serial logging at 115200 baud reports ultrasonic readings, IR state changes, servo targets and motor commands. Motor electrical ratings, servo loads and real landing accuracy still need physical calibration.
